### Export an OpenFF OpenMM System to AMBER and GROMACS files

- **Repository:** <https://github.com/openforcefield/openff-toolkit/tree/stable/examples/using_smirnoff_in_amber_or_gromacs>
- **Licenses:** MIT
- **Tags:** SMIRNOFF, Interchange, AMBER, OpenMM
- **Languages:** Python
- **Project:** Open Force Field
